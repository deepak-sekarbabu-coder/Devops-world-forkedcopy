# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DEVOPS-WORLD is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome questions asked in good faith.
- Critique ideas and technical work, not people.
- Support claims with evidence when possible.
- Accept correction professionally.
- Respect privacy, licenses, and intellectual property.
- Help keep discussions relevant and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, insults, intimidation, or deliberate disruption
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Plagiarism, impersonation, or deceptive attribution
- Repeated promotional spam or off-topic solicitation
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other official DEVOPS-WORLD community spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the private contact method listed on the VERIQTA GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive reports in a public issue.

Include relevant links, screenshots, dates, and a short description of what happened. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. The response will depend on the severity, impact, and history of the behavior.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
